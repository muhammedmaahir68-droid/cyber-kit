### 💡 Slide 2: Proposed Solution & 6 Operational Modules
![Slide 2: Proposed Solution & 6 Operational Modules](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/muhammedmaahir68-droid/cyber-kit/main/docs/slides/Slide2_SIH189.jpg)

* **MOD-01 (Live Surveillance)**: Sub-50ms live camera ingestion, OpenCV face detection, and WebSocket telemetry stream.
* **MOD-02 (Digital Forensics & SIH26150 DVR)**: 131,072 sector carving with Hailo NPU (26 TOPS) and multi-vendor DVR/NVR parser (Hikvision, Dahua, CP Plus).
* **MOD-03 (GNN Syndicate Intel)**: Spectral Graph Convolutional Networks (GCN) mapping cross-border links with 98.6% link precision and Betweenness Centrality kingpin isolation.
* **MOD-04 (ERSS Patrol Mesh)**: Interactive GIS vector map with touch tracking, Haversine ground distance computation, dynamic ETA, and rapid dispatch.
* **MOD-05 (Tactical Hardware Terminal)**: Physical write-blocker bus switch (NVMe, SATA, USB 3.2, JTAG) with raw bitstream acquisition.
* **MOD-06 (Case Copilot Voice AI)**: Siri-style independent voice agent with dynamic acoustic soundwaves, multilingual support (EN/HI/TA), missing evidence radar, and automated statutory requisition generation under BNSS 2023 Sec 94/107/176.
